## Physical Settings

### The Mithraeum (Temple)
- Underground or cave-like structure
- Rectangular room with vaulted ceiling
- Side benches (podia) for ritual dining
- Central aisle
- Main altar with Tauroctony
- Astronomical alignments
- Torch holders and light effects

### Architectural Elements
- Standard orientation based on cosmic model
- Natural cave adaptation where possible
- Rectangular room displaying cosmic polarities
- Size typically accommodating 30-40 people

## Ritual Ceremonies

### The Seven Grades of Initiation

1. **Corax (Raven) - Mercury**
   - Black robes
   - Ritual mask
   - Serves others

2. **Nymphus (Bridegroom) - Venus**
   - White garments
   - Carries lamp
   - Veil-wearing

3. **Miles (Soldier) - Mars**
   - Military equipment
   - Sword and crown ritual
   - Refuses the crown

4. **Leo (Lion) - Jupiter**
   - Red robes
   - Fire rituals
   - Honey purification

5. **Perses (Persian) - Moon**
   - Persian costume
   - Harvest rituals
   - Star symbolism

6. **Heliodromus (Sun-runner) - Sun**
   - Golden crown
   - Solar whip
   - Light ceremonies

7. **Pater (Father) - Saturn**
   - Phrygian cap
   - Staff of authority
   - Leads ceremonies
